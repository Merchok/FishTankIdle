# Reference

All the exact numbers in one place. If you're new, start with [[Game Mechanics]] instead.

All times are real minutes and hours, because the game runs in real time.

## Life stages

| Stage | Age | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fry (Shrimplet, Ephyra) | 0 to 45 min | Earns nothing, hungers fastest |
| Juvenile | 45 min to 3 h | Earns half income |
| Adult | 3 h and up | Full income, can breed |

Creatures you buy arrive as adults. Babies start as fry.

## Care

### Hunger

- Hunger falls from 100 to 0 in about **10 hours** for an average adult. Fry hunger 30% faster, juveniles 10% faster. Some species are hungrier (Bubble Goldie) or less hungry (shrimp, jellies).
- **Feed** drops 7 flakes. Each flake a creature eats gives **+12 hunger**. You can't feed while 30+ flakes are already floating.
- Flakes nobody eats sink and add **0.35 waste** each, so don't overfeed.
- Below **25** hunger a creature takes damage and stops earning coins.
- **Auto feeder** (100 coins): holds up to 12 portions (starts with 6, refill 6 for 12 coins). It feeds anyone under 35 hunger (+60) and adds a little waste.

### Water

- **Waste** rises with the number and size of creatures (fry 0.35, juvenile 0.7, adult 1.0 per space used). A cleaner filter, a bigger tank and plants slow it down.
- Most creatures start taking damage above **55 waste**. Jellyfish are fussier: **40**.
- **Change water** (free): waste drops to 12% of what it was (never below 3).
- **Filter** wears out over time: sponge 30 h, hang-on 48 h, canister 72 h. A worn filter lets waste build up much faster. **Rinse filter** (free) resets it to 100%.
- **Plants** each cut waste growth by 3%.

### Algae

- Algae slowly grows on the glass, faster when the water is dirty. Above **85** it hurts creatures a little.
- **Scrub glass** (free) clears it completely.
- Algae eaters help: Sucker Pleco, all shrimp (Amano is the best), and the Upside-down Jelly nibbles a bit. Only juveniles and adults eat algae.

### Temperature

- Set the heater from **16 to 32 °C**. The water moves toward it by about **1 °C every 10 minutes**.
- Every species has a comfy range. More than 0.5 °C outside it causes damage (up to 6 °C off counts).
- Hybrids tolerate the combined range of both parents.

### Health, sickness and mood

- Damage comes from starving, dirty water, heavy algae and bad temperature. When none of those apply, creatures slowly heal.
- Under **35 health** a creature gets **sick**: it earns no coins and can't breed. It recovers on its own at 60 health, or you can use **Medicine** (15 coins) to cure it right away (sets health to 70).
- **Creatures never die.** They only get sick or unhappy.
- Mood shown on a creature: Sick, Starving (<25 hunger), Hungry (<45), Stressed (waste a bit over the limit), Chilly or hot, Content, or Happy (healthy, fed and the water is clean).

## Money

- A creature earns **10% of its value per hour** as an adult, **5%** as a juvenile. Sick, fry and starving creatures earn **0**.
- Coins pile up on the sand. Tap the pile or press **Collect** to bank them.
- **Value** = base price × (1 + 0.6 × rarity). Rare colors, patterns, tails, fins, sheen and extreme sizes all raise rarity, so a rare morph earns and sells for more than a plain one.
- **Hybrids** have a base price of (average of both parents' prices) × 1.5.
- **Selling** pays 30% of value for fry, 45% for juveniles and 60% for adults. Tap sell twice to confirm.

## Breeding

- Both parents must be the **same kind** (fish, shrimp or jellyfish), **adults**, **not sick** and have **30+ hunger**.
- After breeding, both parents rest for **2 hours**.
- The **nursery** holds **2** clutches at a time.
- Clutch size: fish 2 to 4, shrimp 3 to 6, jellyfish 1 to 2 (2 to 3 if both parents are Comb Jellies).
- Eggs hatch after **30 min** (fish), **40 min** (shrimp) or **45 min** (jellyfish). Two Comb Jellies take 35 min. Babies wait in the nursery if the tank is full.
- Each baby gets one of each parent's two copies of every trait. Every trait has about a **2%** chance to mutate into something new (often a rare one).
- Two different species of the same kind make a **hybrid**. Each baby is one of the two species with the other as its hybrid half. Hybrids are **sterile**.

### Jellyfish polyps

- Jellyfish eggs (except Comb Jelly, which hatches straight into fry) settle as **polyps**.
- Polyp spots depend on your tank: Nano 2, Standard 3, Large 4, Show 5.
- Polyps grow slowly (waste must be under 60). Once a polyp is big enough and the water has been at or below its species' **cold** temperature for **3 hours**, it releases 1 to 4 baby jellies.
- The Immortal Jelly turns back into a polyp instead of getting sick.
