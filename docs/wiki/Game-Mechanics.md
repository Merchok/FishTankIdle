# Game Mechanics

New to Finlings? This page is the friendly version. If you want every exact number, there's a [[Reference]] page for that.

## The idea in 30 seconds

You look after a cozy little aquarium. Your creatures **earn coins**, and you **spend coins** on more creatures and a nicer tank. That's it!

The game runs in real time, so one minute in the game is one real minute, even while the app is closed.

You start with **60 coins**, a small tank and **2 Pebble Tetras**.

## Your first few minutes

1. **Collect your coins.** They pile up on the sand. Tap the pile (or press **Collect**) to bank them.
2. **Feed** your fish when they look hungry.
3. **Save up** and buy your next creature in the shop.

You don't need to do anything else yet. The game will nudge you when something needs attention.

## Keeping everyone happy

Four things matter. Happy creatures earn coins, unhappy ones don't.

| Thing | What to do | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Food** | Press **Feed** | When hunger gets low. Don't overfeed, leftover food makes the water dirty |
| **Water** | Press **Change water** (free) | Before the waste meter gets high. Most creatures suffer above **55** |
| **Glass** | Press **Scrub glass** (free) | When algae gets high. It starts to hurt above **85** |
| **Temperature** | Set the heater | Match your creatures' comfy range (the start setting of 25 °C suits Pebble Tetras) |

Your filter slowly wears out too. **Rinse filter** is free and makes it good as new.

### What if something goes wrong?

Don't worry, **creatures never die**. If they're hungry, in dirty water or too cold or hot, they get unhappy and eventually **sick**. A sick creature earns nothing, but it gets better by itself once you fix the problem. **Medicine** (15 coins) cures it right away.

Each creature shows a **mood** (Happy, Hungry, Chilly, Sick and so on), which tells you what's wrong.

## Making coins

- Adults earn about **10% of their value every hour**. Young ones earn less, and babies earn nothing yet.
- Rarer colors and patterns are worth more, so they earn more.
- Sick or starving creatures earn nothing.
- Don't want a creature any more? You can **sell** it. Adults sell for 60% of their value.

## Growing up

Creatures go through three stages:

- **Fry** (first 45 minutes): too small to earn.
- **Juvenile** (up to 3 hours old): earns half.
- **Adult** (3 hours and up): earns in full, and can breed.

Creatures you buy in the shop are already adults.

## What to buy

Here's a good order. It's only a suggestion, so do what feels fun!

1. **More creatures** until your tank is full. Each one takes up space.
2. **An auto feeder** (100 coins) feeds hungry creatures for you. Great for when you're away.
3. **A bigger tank** when you run out of room. Bigger tanks also stay clean longer.
4. **A better filter** to keep the water clean for longer.
5. **Plants and decorations.** Plants help keep the water clean, the rest just look nice.

Algae-eaters (the Sucker Pleco and all the shrimp) are handy helpers, because they clean the glass for you.

## Breeding

Breeding is how you get new colors and patterns, and it's the heart of the collection book.

**To breed two creatures they need to be:**

- Both **adults**, and the same kind (fish with fish, shrimp with shrimp, jellyfish with jellyfish).
- Healthy and fed (not sick, hunger 30 or more).
- Not resting. After breeding, a pair needs a **2 hour** break.

**What happens:**

1. Pick two creatures on the breeding screen. It shows you the chances for each trait.
2. They lay eggs in the **nursery**, which holds 2 batches at once.
3. After about **30 to 45 minutes** the eggs hatch into fry. You need free space in the tank for them.

The babies get a mix of their parents' traits. Now and then a baby gets a brand new look no parent had.

**Two different species?** Same kind, different species gives you a **hybrid**. Hybrids are special, but they're sterile (they can't breed).

**Jellyfish are a bit different.** Their eggs become tiny **polyps** that stick to the tank (you have a few polyp spots, more in bigger tanks). When the water is **cold** enough for a few hours, the polyps release baby jellies. Each jelly species has its own cold temperature. Comb Jellies skip all of this and just lay eggs.

## When you're away

The game catches up when you come back, for up to **72 hours** away. Your creatures keep earning, they might get hungry, but they can't die. Your tank is saved on your own device only, so use **Backup** in Settings if you want to move it.
